Privacy Policy

Zoe Life LLC

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

At Zoe Life LLC, we value your trust and respect your privacy. We are committed to handling the personal information you share with us responsibly and with care.

This Privacy Policy explains how Zoe Life LLC (“Zoe Life,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit our website, www.zoelifehub.com, contact us, subscribe to our communications, purchase our products, or engage with our services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Zoe Life, we may collect the following information:

Contact Information: Your name, email address, phone number, and other contact details you voluntarily provide.

Coaching and Consultation Information: Information you provide when requesting a consultation, completing an inquiry form, scheduling a session, or participating in our coaching services.

Purchase Information: Information related to purchases of books, digital resources, and other products or services. Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

Newsletter Information: Your email address and subscription preferences when you sign up to receive communications from Zoe Life.

Website Usage Information: Technical information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and other usage data that may be collected through cookies or website analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

Respond to questions, inquiries, and requests.

Schedule consultations and coaching sessions.

Provide coaching, educational resources, and other services.

Process purchases and communicate about orders.

Send newsletters, encouragement, helpful resources, announcements, and updates when you have subscribed.

Improve our website, products, resources, and services.

Maintain appropriate business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information to third-party marketers.

3. Coaching Information and Confidentiality

We recognize that individuals, couples, and families may share personal or sensitive information when seeking coaching, encouragement, or guidance.

We seek to handle such information with care, discretion, and respect for your privacy.

Information shared through coaching inquiries and services is handled in accordance with our applicable coaching agreements and privacy practices. Confidentiality may be subject to legal obligations, safety considerations, and other exceptions described in the relevant coaching agreement.

We encourage visitors not to submit highly sensitive personal information through general website contact forms.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to support our website and business operations, including:

Website hosting and maintenance.

Appointment scheduling and virtual meetings.

Payment processing.

Email communications and newsletter management.

Product sales and digital resource delivery.

Website analytics and security.

These providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party platforms when using their services.

5. Email Communications

If you subscribe to our mailing list, we may send you encouragement, helpful resources, announcements, and updates about Zoe Life’s books, services, events, and other offerings.

You may unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the unsubscribe link provided in our emails or by contacting us through the Contact Us form on our website.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, understand visitor interactions, and improve the user experience.

Depending on the technologies used, cookies may help us remember preferences, monitor website performance, or understand how visitors use our website.

Where applicable, visitors may be able to manage cookie preferences through their browser settings or available website controls.

7. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain appropriate business records, fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we maintain about you, subject to applicable laws and legitimate business or legal requirements.

You may also unsubscribe from our emails at any time.

To make a privacy-related request, please use the Contact Us form on our website.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended primarily for adults, including parents and guardians seeking resources for themselves and their families.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 through our website without appropriate parental consent.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

11. External Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including social media platforms, retailers, and scheduling or payment services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of websites that we do not operate.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of external websites before sharing personal information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, website practices, or legal requirements.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle personal information, please reach out through the Contact Us form on our website.
