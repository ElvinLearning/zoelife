About Zoe Life

Helping People Thrive in Every Season of Life

Zoe Life equips individuals, couples, families, churches, and organizations with biblical truth, practical wisdom, and Christ-centered resources to help them thrive in every season of life.

The meaning of Zoe

Life as God intends it.

Zoe comes from the Greek word for life. In Scripture it often refers to the fullness of life that comes from God, not simply being alive but living well, with purpose and hope.

"I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly." John 10:10, NKJV

That abundant life is the heart of this organization. Zoe Life exists to help people experience it, whatever season they are in.

Mission

Biblical and practical.

Zoe Life equips individuals, couples, families, churches, and organizations with biblical truth, practical wisdom, and Christ-centered resources to help them thrive in every season of life.

Zoe Life starts with what Scripture says, then works out what it means in practice, through thoughtful questions, biblically grounded guidance, and practical tools.

Vision

Lives changed by the truth of God's Word.

  • Individuals growing spiritually
  • Marriages strengthened
  • Families flourishing
  • Leaders serving with wisdom and integrity
  • Churches equipped for ministry
  • Communities positively affected

Meet the founders

Tayo and Kemi Akinyemi

Pastors Tayo and Kemi Akinyemi are the founders of Zoe Life and pastors of Life Springs Church.

They are founders, pastors, coaches, mentors, parents, and professionals. Their work brings together ministry experience, professional leadership, teaching, mentoring, coaching, and lifelong learning.

Their public focus includes relationships, family life, leadership, education, careers, stewardship, and personal growth through Scripture.
