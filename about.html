About

About Zoe Life

Helping people thrive in every season of life.

Zoe Life was established out of our shared passion for God and people, and our desire to encourage, equip, and empower individuals, couples, and families to thrive in every season of life.

We believe that God’s Word provides the foundation for living a purposeful and fulfilling life. Through faith-based and practical coaching, teaching, books, and other resources, our desire is to help people grow in their relationship with God, develop personally, strengthen relationships, and navigate life’s opportunities and challenges with wisdom and confidence.

The name Zoe comes from the Greek word for life, used in John 10:10, where Jesus speaks of the abundant life He came to give. This Scripture reflects the heart behind Zoe Life. We desire to help people discover, experience, and walk in the fullness of life that God has made available through Jesus Christ.

Whether you’re seeking personal growth, guidance in relationships or marriage, support in parenting, or direction in education or career development, our heart is to provide encouragement, biblical wisdom, and practical resources that support you along the way.

Mission

Encouraging, Equipping, and Empowering People to Thrive

Our mission is to provide faith-based and practical coaching, teaching, encouragement, and resources that help individuals, couples, and families grow in faith, develop personally, strengthen relationships, make wise decisions, and thrive in every season of life.

Vision

Lives Transformed, Families Strengthened, and Communities Impacted

Our vision is to see individuals experiencing the abundant life that Jesus Christ came to give, growing in their relationship with God, and fulfilling His purpose for their lives. We desire to see marriages and families strengthened, individuals thriving in every season of life, and people making a positive difference in their homes, workplaces, churches, and communities.

Who we are

Core values

Christ-Centered

Jesus Christ is the foundation of all we do. We desire to honor Him and point others toward Him through our work.

Biblical Truth

God’s Word is our foundation and final authority. We are committed to sharing biblical truth faithfully and encouraging its application in everyday life.

Practical Wisdom

We believe biblical principles should be lived out. Our desire is to help people apply God’s Word and practical wisdom to everyday decisions and experiences.

Excellence

We are committed to honoring God through excellence, diligence, and faithful service in all we do.

Integrity

We value honesty, accountability, and consistency between what we believe, teach, and practice.

Compassion

We desire to serve others with genuine love, kindness, patience, and understanding.

Stewardship

We seek to faithfully manage the time, opportunities, gifts, and resources God has entrusted to us.

Growth

We believe in continually growing in our relationship with God, developing personally, and becoming more effective in serving others.

Meet Tayo and Kemi Akinyemi

Tayo and Kemi Akinyemi are beloved children of God, husband and wife, parents of six children, pastors, and cofounders of Zoe Life LLC. Married for nearly twenty years, they serve together in pastoral leadership at Life Springs Church in Naperville, Illinois.

Their shared passion for God and people, together with their experiences in marriage, parenting, ministry, education, leadership, and their professional careers, inspired them to establish Zoe Life as a platform to encourage, equip, and empower others.

Through Zoe Life, they bring together biblical wisdom, practical insights, and personal and professional experiences to support individuals, couples, and families as they grow in faith, strengthen relationships, pursue their goals, and thrive in every season of life.

Our Statement of Faith

At Zoe Life, our work is grounded in historic Christian faith and the authority of God's Word. We believe:

  • The Bible is the inspired, authoritative, and trustworthy Word of God.
  • There is one God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully man, whose life, death, and resurrection provide salvation for all who place their faith in Him.
  • Salvation is by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ and cannot be earned by human effort.
  • The Holy Spirit indwells every believer, empowering holy living, spiritual growth, and faithful service.
  • Every person is created in the image of God and possesses inherent dignity and value.
  • The Church is the body of Christ, called to worship God, make disciples, and demonstrate His love to the world.
  • Marriage is God's covenant between one man and one woman and is designed to reflect Christ's relationship with His Church.
  • God calls believers to lives of holiness, integrity, compassion, stewardship, and faithful obedience.
  • Jesus Christ will return, and God's Kingdom will be fully established according to His promises.

Everything we teach, create, and provide through Zoe Life seeks to be consistent with these biblical convictions.
