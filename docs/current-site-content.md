## Brand promise

Helping People Thrive in Every Season of Life.

Zoe Life equips individuals, couples, families, churches, and organizations with biblical truth, practical wisdom, and Christ-centered resources to help them thrive in every season of life.

The name Zoe comes from the Greek word for life, often referring in Scripture to the fullness of life that comes from God. The current site cites John 10:10 (NKJV) and describes abundant life as the heart of the organization.

## Meeting and email direction to preserve

The private concept should reflect what was discussed and later sent by the client:

- Make "every season of life" an actual organizing idea, not just a tagline.
- Let visitors identify a current season: building, waiting, growing, healing, or leading.
- Organize the offer into three clear pathways: Relationships and Family, Academic and Career, Faith and Life Resources.
- Provide useful guidance before promoting services or products.
- Invite reflection and conversation.
- Position Tayo and Kemi as teachers, coaches, and encouragers.
- Show men, fathers, husbands, couples, students, and professionals in the visual mix. Avoid an overly feminine design.
- Use warm orange, peach, tan, and brown as source brand colors, but do not copy the Dear Pastor's Wife visual system or page rhythm.
- Use coaching, not counseling.
- A free 20-minute consultation was discussed but remains unconfirmed. Do not present it as available.
