In this series we have been talking about the right one. The right one is not necessarily the perfect one, but the right one for *you*. The right one doesn't have to be the most attractive one, at least not at first. And the right one is God's plan for your marriage. We also talked about how you can recognize the right one: through divine revelation, through spiritual and natural authority, in the place of your service, through the company God has placed around you, as you pursue purpose, through your culture, and through divine providence. Hallelujah!

So now let's assume you have met the right one. What do you do?

It's one thing to find the right one. It's another thing to connect successfully with the right one. In our years of pastoring, there have been times we were convinced a relationship was of God, and it never even started. Others started and never made it to marriage. Just because it is the right one does not mean it will automatically move forward.

So here are three things to do.

## 1. Get Divine Confirmation

If you believe this person is the right one, the first thing you need is confirmation from God. This is very important.

Now, if God spoke to you, that is different. But these days you need to be careful when people say, "God spoke to me." Some people have not exercised themselves enough spiritually, so what they are calling God is really their own soul talking to them, not the Spirit of God. You can be attracted to something that is not God. In fact, you can be attracted to something *good* and it is still not God's will for you. So pray. Test it out.

I heard a man of God say, "There are certain things I don't do unless God tells me." Marriage is one of those things. Going into a relationship is one of those things. You don't just jump in.

Think about David. It came into his heart to build a house for God, and he told Nathan the prophet. Nathan said, "That's a great idea. Go, God is with you." But that same night, the Lord spoke to Nathan: as good as David's idea was, it was not God's will for his life. Some people run into someone nice. That doesn't mean that person is God's plan for your life.

This matters so much, because every relationship and every marriage will be tested. When the test comes, if you don't know God is in it, the way you fight is different. You need to know God is in it.

### If God told you, you don't have to tell them

We want to go off on a little tangent here, because this is important. If God has shown you this person is to be your spouse, you don't have to go and tell them, "God told me." In fact, you really shouldn't. Let the God who told you speak to their heart. Let the God who told you work out a way to make the connection happen.

You can put out signals. But there is no need to put that person under the bondage of, "If I don't say yes, something bad is going to happen to me." You don't want to marry somebody under duress, or put somebody under duress.

And the other way around: if somebody comes to you and says, "God told me you are my wife," or "God told me you are my husband," go hear from the Lord for yourself. You cannot live off of somebody else's revelation. This can be tricky, especially when it comes from someone you love and respect, maybe someone you see as a spiritual mentor. Let the Holy Spirit lead the process. Don't get ahead of God.

Usually one person sees it before the other. We did meet a couple recently who said they met each other and both just knew. But typically one sees first, and that's okay. The one who sees first should give the other person time, and the other person should take the time to seek the Lord.

Even when God uses an authority in your life to ask, "Have you considered this person?", you still need to hear from God yourself. Those authorities are pointers. You are still responsible for your decision, so be sincere before God.

### God speaks the way He usually speaks to you

What concerns us is the person who has never heard God about anything in their life, and suddenly hears Him very clearly about marriage. God is a God of order: line upon line, precept upon precept. He will generally use what He normally uses with you. When David went to fight Goliath, he didn't use Saul's armor. He used what he had tested and what he knew, and God blessed it.

### Look for grace

One of the ways God confirms something is grace. If you are in a relationship and there is no grace, God is not there. When there is grace, even the tough parts are doable. When there is no grace, even what should be easy is tough. Learn to perceive the grace of God, so you can say, "Okay, I'm doing the right thing."

### God confirms to both of you

Look at Mary. The angel came, she had her encounter with the Lord, and she said, "Be it unto me according to your word." Then she went and told Joseph, and Joseph was like, "This thing you're telling me?" But God didn't expect Joseph to live off of Mary's revelation. God confirmed it to Joseph too. He had his own encounter: "Do not be afraid to take to you Mary your wife." God is in the business of speaking to both believers. (And as we have this conversation, we are speaking about a believer marrying another believer.)

Please hear this: this area of your life is too important for God not to confirm it. God has a vested interest in your marriage. It is a lot harder to do the will of God married to the wrong person, and a lot easier married to the right one. Your marriage also shapes your children and the next generation. Some people make such a mess that it takes God ten, fifteen years to fix it before their children can start building. But when you do it right, your children start on a different platform and simply build from what you have done.

So God will confirm. God is *eager* to confirm. Seek the Lord, wait on the Lord, and pray.

## 2. Consecrate Yourself

Sometimes you perceive in your heart, "This is it," but your flesh gets in the way. Maybe this isn't the one you like. Maybe someone else has been giving you attention. If you find yourself there, pray a prayer of consecration: *Lord, I consecrate myself to Your will. I consecrate myself to Your plan.* That is what Jesus prayed. He knew the will of the Father, and His flesh was saying, "Do I really have to go through this?" And He said, "Not My will, but Yours be done."

We have to get to the place where we fully trust God, because He sees far ahead.

**Kemi:** I tell people all the time, I didn't know I was going to need this kind of husband for the things I have had to do, in my career and in ministry. You don't see that far ahead. You get glimpses. But God sees far out.

Think of it like present value and future value. God sees where He wants you to be in the future, and He shows you the present value right now. The present value might not look so good. But with the interest, and the growth, and the journey you will walk with this person, the future value will be glorious. Then there are other relationships where the present value looks really good, but they are actually at their peak and you don't even know it.

> But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day.
> Proverbs 4:18 (KJV)

When you do it God's way, your marriage should get better, your relationship should get better, your friendship should get deeper over time. The way of the enemy is, "The best is now," and then everything depreciates. So don't be moved when you see some people celebrating something that looks glorious but isn't God. Give it time. Sometimes in five years they are not even together. When it's not God, it gets worse over time. When it's God, it gets better and better. That's what you want.

### When your taste has been infected

Some of us have a problem: our taste has been infected. What we think is good is actually not good, and what we think is bad is actually good. Sometimes it comes from being raised in the wrong environment, or exposing ourselves to the wrong information. Romance movies and romance novels have damaged a lot of couples by creating unrealistic expectations. On the men's side, pornography does real damage. Then people wonder what is wrong with the person God brought them. No, it's you. You need to flush all that junk out of your system.

So if God has revealed and confirmed someone and you are not feeling it, pray: *Lord, help me love Your will.* It might take a few months. The person God brings may not be your "natural taste," but your natural taste might be the problem. Trust God above what you think.

## 3. Pray, and Don't Pressure

Sometimes you know this is the Lord, you have put out a signal or two, and the other person just isn't seeing it. Don't be in a hurry to move on. Spend time in prayer: *Lord, ultimately I desire Your perfect will and Your perfect plan.* And pray a selfless prayer while you're at it: *Lord, I pray for Your perfect will for this person.* That way, if it's not you, or if you are the one missing it, the Lord will work it out.

One thing we have come to realize: the more you pray, if it is God, it tends to grow on you. If it's not God, it tends to fade.

Praying in the Holy Spirit is a very good place to begin, because the Holy Spirit helps you pray the perfect will of God for that situation. But whatever you do, pray, pray, pray. When something is the will of God, prayer creates the infrastructure for people to connect. One person is in one city, the other is in another city, and they need to meet. When you begin to pray, there is angelic activity that brings the right situations and circumstances together for a divine connection. Don't underestimate prayer.

**Put pressure on God, not on the person.** Even if you are convinced God spoke to you, even if an angel appeared to you, don't put pressure on the person. Jesus said, "Come to Me." So go to God, and let God deal with them. The truth is, you don't want somebody in a relationship with you because they *have to* be. That is not God's blessing. You want them there because they want to be, and the only way they will want to is if they are convinced it's God.

Some people know it's God and still aren't acting right. Keep praying for them, and God will work with them. And if they still refuse, remember: God's plan for you is not locked into one person. God will not hold your life hostage forever. He can bring somebody better.

If you will learn to seek God for divine confirmation, consecrate yourself, and pray for the other person, most of the time you will get a clear "go ahead." Or a clear "stop, stop, stop." Thank God for the stops!

We were talking recently to a lady who had someone pursuing her, and some people were putting pressure on her to say yes. But the more she prayed, the more she knew it wasn't him, and she held her ground. A few years later, that man passed away. She said, "If I had married him, I would be a young widow," maybe with one or two children to raise alone. God knows the future.

So learn to follow God. If God says go, go. If God says no, it's no. Don't twist God's arm. You can't anyway, so don't try! And sometimes He says, "Hold on. I'm working a few things out." Just go with His leading.

::: takeaways Three Steps After You Meet the Right One
- **Get divine confirmation.** Hear from God for yourself, and look for the evidence of His grace.
- **Consecrate yourself.** Pray, "Not my will, but Yours," and ask God to help you love His will.
- **Pray instead of pressuring.** Pray selflessly for the other person and let God work on their heart.
:::
