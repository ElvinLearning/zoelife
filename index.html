About us — the actual people

Hi. We’re Kemi & Tayo.

Two pastors, one family, and a stubborn conviction that the abundant life Jesus talked about isn’t a theory. It’s Tuesday-night dinner. It’s marriages that tell the truth. It’s kids who feel seen, and work that actually means something.

Pastor Kemi

Writer · Teacher · The one with the book

Kemi has spent two decades helping people trade survival mode for the zoē kind of living — and finally wrote it all down. She’ll feed you before you leave. It’s not optional.
