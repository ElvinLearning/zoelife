Recipes for a Blessed Marriage: It Starts With the Fear of God

By Tayo and Kemi · October 13, 2026 · 8 min read

In this article

  • Marriage Is Supposed to Be a Blessing
  • Why We Call Them Recipes
  • The Foundation: Submitting to One Another in the Fear of God
  • A Word to the Singles: Choose Wisely
  • Before Our Next Recipe
  • Let's Pray

Blessed marriages don't just show up out of the blue! There are tools God has given us in His Word for a blessed marriage, and in this series we want to explore them together. Married couples, this is for you. And singles, you can learn a lot here too. In fact, you have an advantage: you get to learn these things early, so you know what to do from day one, and you know who to choose.

Marriage Is Supposed to Be a Blessing

God created marriage, and we have to start from that foundation. Everything God created in Genesis 1, He called good. But when He first created Adam, He said, "It is not good that man should be alone." Then He created them male and female, blessed them, told them to be fruitful, and called it all very good. Man in union. Man married. That is what God called very good.

When we listen to people, some of them see marriage as a necessary evil: "We just have to endure it." That is not what God created. You should enjoy your marriage! That doesn't mean there won't be challenges. But overall, you should get far more out of your marriage than you put in.

Why We Call Them Recipes

We have been married many years now, and through those years there have been challenges we've had to grow through and overcome. Challenges with some tears, salt and pepper, tears for seasoning! What keeps us going is going back to the foundations. Back to the recipes. What does the Word say? How do we navigate this by the Word? If one of us isn't feeling loved, how does the Word tell us to respond? If one of us isn't feeling respected, how would the Spirit of God have us respond in that moment?

That's why we call them recipes. You want to make bread, so you have your flour, your eggs, your butter. But it's got to be mixed! It's not enough for a wife to know the Bible says, "Wives, submit to your own husbands, as to the Lord." That's great. But she needs to understand how to work it, especially in the face of real situations. Do I need this much flour, or just a little? If you add too much of one ingredient, what is supposed to be sweet turns out too salty. You need the right balance of all the ingredients.

That matters, because people who don't understand this sometimes come to a false conclusion: "What God said doesn't work. What God said isn't practical. What God said is oppressive." We hear all kinds of thinking like that, even among believers. But God's Word is true, God's Word is right, and God's Word is a blessing. If you see something in God's Word that you feel is not right, be humble enough to say, "The problem might be with me, not with God." That positions you to receive.

The Foundation: Submitting to One Another in the Fear of God

When people read the marriage scriptures, they usually start with Ephesians 5:22. That is very important. But we want to go back one verse, because if Ephesians 5:21 isn't in place, Ephesians 5:22 will be a lot harder to live out.

Submitting to one another in the fear of God.

Ephesians 5:21 (NKJV)

God's best is for a mature believing man to marry a mature believing woman. You should be a good man before you are a good husband. In fact, we don't think you can biblically be a good husband if you are not first a good man. There is a progression. And before anyone steps from being an individual to being married, there is a submission principle every believer has to embrace.

Sometimes we see women marry men who have never embraced this principle, and then they ask, "Why should I submit to him?" We understand. But part of the problem is that you should marry someone who is already a good man, so that he becomes a good husband.

Every believer has a head

But I want you to know that the head of every man is Christ, the head of woman is man, and the head of Christ is God.

1 Corinthians 11:3 (NKJV)

In the Greek, the words for man and husband are the same, and so are the words for woman and wife. You read the context to know which is meant, and here the context is marriage. So the first thing this verse tells us is that every believing husband has a head. As a husband, he can't just do what he wants. He is submitted to Christ. And look at the end of the verse: the head of Christ is God. In His earthly life and ministry, Jesus submitted to the Father. If Jesus had to submit, who are any of us to think we won't?

Hebrews tells us Jesus' prayers were heard because of His godly fear. He reverenced the Father so much that He said He only spoke what He heard the Father say. That is a high level of reverence.

Tayo: Sometimes I run into Christians who make certain statements, and I ask, "Have you checked with God before you said that?" I've come up with a policy, and people think I'm joking, but I try not to have an opinion when I don't know what God has said. I don't want to be found saying something that is against God, something the Father has not said.

So Ephesians 5:21 belongs to everybody. Husbands submit. Wives submit. Believers submit. Apostles and prophets submit. When people don't understand this, they think submission is a special suffering reserved for wives. You can hear it in some of their questions. But in this family, in this kingdom, we submit to one another. We don't just do our own thing. There is a line of authority we follow. And we do it because we fear God.

Why the fear of God makes the recipe work

Kemi: This context is so important. As you go through the rest of Ephesians 5, you see the instructions for husbands and for wives. When God says to me, "Kemi, submit to your husband Tayo," it's in the fear of God. My submission to him is in the fear of God, and that is why I can submit even when he isn't quite there yet. And it works the other way too. His loving me is in the fear of God, so even if my submission is a little short, he is still obligated to love me, in the fear of God.

That's why this recipe works best for people who are committed to walking in the fear of God. Sometimes one spouse fears God and the other doesn't, and that creates problems. We will talk in this series about how to handle that, because there is nothing new under the sun, and the Bible deals with it. But God's ideal, what brings out the best flavor, is when both husband and wife have that mindset: "I'm submitting to one another. I'm accountable. I'm not just doing my own thing, because I fear God."

Sometimes we see people get married with no sense of responsibility and no sense of accountability. When we hear people struggling to love their wives or respect their husbands, so often we just don't see the fear of the Lord in them. If you fear the Lord, there are certain things you won't say and certain things you won't do, or at least you won't make them your lifestyle.

A Word to the Singles: Choose Wisely

Do not be unequally yoked together with unbelievers. For what fellowship has righteousness with lawlessness? And what communion has light with darkness?

2 Corinthians 6:14 (NKJV)

Some people read this and think God is just being hard: "But I'm in love with her! I'm in love with him!" But marriage is supposed to work on certain principles, on certain recipes. It works when we do it God's way. Starting out unequally yoked is starting on the wrong foundation.

So to those of you who are single and still have the opportunity to choose: choose wisely. Be equally yoked with God-loving, God-fearing believers. Look for someone who says, "Lord, whether I'm single or married, my desire is to please You and live in the fear of the Lord." And if you run into someone who isn't willing to submit to anyone, they are not marriage material.

Before Our Next Recipe

  • Expect a blessing. Marriage is not a necessary evil. God designed it to give you far more than you put in.
  • Go back to the Word when you hit a challenge: how does Scripture tell you to respond right here?
  • Check your foundation. Are you submitting to one another in the fear of God, or only asking your spouse to?
  • Singles, look for the fear of the Lord in a potential spouse before anything else.

Let's Pray

Heavenly Father, we pray for Your people. In the name of Jesus, restore the fear of God to those who have let it slip. You are the most important One. Your Word is the highest, and Your name is the greatest. You are the One we submit to. Let that impartation be released on Your people right now.

And Lord, for those who are not yet married, help them choose wisely. Let them see evidence of the fear of God in a potential spouse before they take that relationship into marriage, because it is such a strong foundation. We give You praise, in Jesus' name. Amen.
