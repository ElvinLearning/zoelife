BOOKS & PUBLICATIONS

Resources to Encourage and Equip You.

We are excited to share books and resources created to encourage you in your walk with God, support your personal growth, and help you apply biblical principles in everyday life.

Our first two publications focus on something very close to our hearts: cultivating and maintaining a lifestyle of thanksgiving to God. We pray that these resources will encourage you to intentionally recognize God’s goodness and make gratitude a part of your daily life.

Devotional

A 7-Day Gratitude Devotional

Cultivating a Heart of Thanksgiving to God

By Kemi Akinyemi

There is so much to be thankful to God for, and sometimes we simply need to be more intentional about recognizing and expressing our gratitude to Him.

This seven-day devotional invites you to spend time in God’s Word, reflect on His goodness, and express your thanksgiving through prayer. Each day provides Scripture, encouragement, reflection, and prayer to help you cultivate a heart of gratitude to God.

Whether you are beginning this journey or looking to strengthen your existing practice of thanksgiving, we pray that this devotional will encourage you to make gratitude to God a part of your everyday life.

Journal

A 100-Day Gratitude Journal

Cultivating a Lifestyle of Thanksgiving to God

By Kemi Akinyemi

God’s goodness and faithfulness give us countless reasons to be thankful, and we believe that taking time to intentionally reflect on them can help us develop a more consistent lifestyle of gratitude.

This 100-day guided journal provides an opportunity to pause each day, reflect on Scripture, consider God’s goodness in your own life, and express your personal thanksgiving to Him.

With daily Scriptures, gratitude reflections, and space to write your own thoughts and prayers, the journal encourages you to develop a habit of thanksgiving while creating a personal record of God’s faithfulness in your life.
